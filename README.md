## About

My name is Laiqian, although almost everyone ends up calling me Lai.

I started messing around with my parents' laptop when I was barely 5 years old.
That curiosity, together with my interest in mathematics, led me to study Data Science, right when AI was changing the way we build and use technology.
Today I work as an AI Engineer, building AI-powered solutions for companies: from LLMs, RAG and agents to classical machine learning.

I like experimenting, building projects and venturing into things I don't know yet.
I also enjoy communicating ideas, creating content and sharing what I learn.

- AI Engineer at **[Solver IA](https://iasolver.es/)**, Valencia.
- BSc Data Science at **UPV**.
- 1st place at the **Mercadona Hackathon** 2025.
- Founder of **Sigma Data Club**.
- Outside of screens: reading, photography and sports.

If you're into AI, technology or simply enjoy exchanging ideas, let's talk.
